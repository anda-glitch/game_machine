How the Bot Thinks

A replay of the bot hunting a hidden fleet, one shot at a time, using the exact code it runs against you. Before every shot it repeats the same four steps.

  1. 1 · EnumerateTry every remaining ship at every start cell in both orientations. Throw out placements that touch a miss or a sunk ship.
  2. 2 · ScoreEach open cell scores the number of surviving placements covering it. If a ship is wounded, only placements through the hits count, weighted heavily.
  3. 3 · RestrictWhile hunting, only cells on a checkerboard spaced by the smallest ship afloat are allowed. The gap widens as small ships sink.
  4. 4 · Fire & updateShoot the highest-scoring allowed cell. The result (miss, hit or sunk) changes which placements survive next turn.

    Two different engines

    The bot (this replay) uses exact counting. It scores each ship's placements separately, recomputes them from scratch every turn, and needs no randomness. The 🔥 Enemy Heatmap in battle and the 🔬 Sim Lab use sampling. A Markov chain moves whole 5-ship fleets around, keeping only fleets with no overlaps that cover every unsunk hit and avoid every miss and sunk cell. After each of your shots it throws its samples away and re-runs the chain with the new evidence.
